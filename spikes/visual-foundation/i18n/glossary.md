# Russian glossary

The lab follows the terms of the course (Теория вероятностей, математическая статистика и комбинаторика, 12 лекций).
Write natural textbook Russian, not a word-for-word translation: short sentences, the reader is a student.

## Style

- Typography: «ёлочки» for quotes, em dash « — » with spaces, `ё` where it belongs (ещё, всё, её, объём, смещённая, Чебышёв).
- Numbers keep the decimal point in the dictionaries; the lab shows the decimal comma by itself. Thousands: `24 000`.
- Formulas: TeX between `$…$` stays as it is. Words inside a formula go into `\text{…}` in Russian:
  `\text{under}\ H_0` → `\text{при}\ H_0`. Never leave Cyrillic in a formula outside `\text{…}`.
- Notation of the course: $M(X)$ expectation, $D(X)$ variance, $\sigma$ standard deviation, $\Phi$, $\Phi_0$ (Laplace function), $C_n^k$, $A_n^k$.
- Instructions address the student politely: «Откройте станцию», «Измените параметр»; never «ты».
- Parameter labels with choices keep their exact shape: `Law (0 normal, 1 uniform)` → `Закон (0 нормальный, 1 равномерный)`
  — same numbers, same separators (`,` `·` `;`), the list in round brackets at the end.
